Platform Features

Engineered with high performance FFmpeg pipelines and robust Django architecture.

1. Vertical Framing Options

  • Center Crop: Scales and crops 16:9 videos directly to 9:16 (1080x1920) filling the entire mobile screen.
  • Blurred Background: Duplicates video stream, blurs the background to fill 1080x1920, and overlays the scaled original video in the center.
  • Fit (Letterbox/Pillarbox): Maintains full aspect ratio and pads margins with black.

2. Configurable Clip Durations

Supports preset YouTube Shorts durations with sub-second accuracy:

15 Seconds 30 Seconds 45 Seconds 60 Seconds 90 Seconds

3. Subtitle Engine

Automatic transcription with Advanced SubStation Alpha (.ass) subtitle burn-in. Choose between Classic White, Bold Viral Yellow, and Opaque Subtitle Box styling.

4. Asynchronous Processing & Celery Architecture

Video probing, encoding, and thumbnail generation run inside isolated background workers, providing real-time job progress polling without blocking web requests.

5. Compliance & Security First

Subprocess argument array execution prevents shell injection; FFprobe validation blocks corrupted or malicious media; quota enforcement prevents storage exhaustion.
